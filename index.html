About our classes

Questions, answered

What is Muraqabah Initiative?

Muraqabah Initiative is a Canadian organization offering traditional Islamic education, spiritual formation, and community service. Classes are taught by qualified teachers trained at Dar al-Mustafa in Tarim, Yemen, and in the academy.

Are classes online or in person?

Most classes are taught live on Zoom so you can join from anywhere. Gardens of Paradise also holds a monthly in-person gathering in Ontario, currently in Guelph.

Who teaches at Muraqabah?

Our teachers are Shaykh Zakaria AbdilAziz, Shaykh Nuh Sanders, and Dr. Ali Hussain.

What classes are open for registration now?

The Art of Seeking with Dr. Ali Hussain begins Saturday, November 28, 2026, and Gardens of Paradise with Shaykh Zakaria meets weekly with a monthly in-person gathering.

Where is Muraqabah based?

We are based in the Greater Toronto Area and Hamilton region of Ontario, and we teach students online around the world.

How can I support the work?

You can give through Support Our Work or Sponsor a Student, both processed securely by Zeffy. Sponsoring a student opens a seat for someone who cannot afford tuition.
